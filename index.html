Administrative

  • Enhancement requests

Escalation

Escalation Process

🔴

P1 Issues — Immediate

Raised via the shared Ad Operations inbox with immediate notification to the Ad Operations Manager and VP Platform Operations. All P1 issues are tracked through our internal ticketing system.

🌙

After-hours P1

Emergency P1 issues outside standard business hours are handled via the designated UK team emergency contact. Please use this route only for critical live campaign failures.

⚠️

SLA Miss

Any missed SLA triggers an automatic notification to the Ad Operations Manager and VP Platform Operations for immediate review and remediation.
